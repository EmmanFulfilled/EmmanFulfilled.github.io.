Oct 2026Group of 4

Healthcare Innovation Challenge

Transforming Health 2026 · TU Dublin and Tallaght University Hospital

Our team designed a prototype equipment tracking system for the hospital’s Occupational Therapy department. It replaces a manual Excel process that was causing lost equipment and slow stock checks.

€4,600roughly what the department was losing in equipment every year

What I did

  • Led design and strategy, turning the clinician’s problem into user flows (collect, return, transfer, repair, mark missing) with a PIN-based staff sign-in that suits a busy ward
  • Built the prototype using AI-assisted development
  • Pitched it to a panel from Amazon and Tallaght University Hospital, who praised the interface as clear and easy to use for non-technical clinical staff
  • User flows
  • Prototyping

Feb – May 2026Group project, TU Dublin

Heart Disease Prediction System

A clinical machine learning system comparing XGBoost, Random Forest and Logistic Regression on a combined dataset of 918 patient records.

  • Handled the full pipeline: data cleaning, preprocessing, training, evaluation and deployment through a Flask web app
  • Random Forest performed best, at 85.3% accuracy
  • Presented the live demo and results to faculty and peers
  • Python
  • scikit-learn
  • XGBoost
  • Flask
